# Region-based annual leave policy

Reviewed on 17 August 2026.

The application counts annual leave in Monday-Friday working days and excludes
regional public holidays separately. Country rules stated in calendar days or in
"working days" that include Saturdays are therefore normalized to this model.

This is a company policy, not a legal calculator. It provides a global floor of
20 working days even where local law requires less (or, in the United States, no
federal paid vacation). Germany uses the requested 30-day company allowance and
Romania starts at 21 days. Legal rules can depend on age, occupation, province,
prior employment, attendance, or collective agreements that the current user
model does not capture; HR must review those cases separately.
